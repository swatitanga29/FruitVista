What these entries mean

  • A purchase adds the bought quantity to the fruit's purchased quantity, so it becomes part of the available stock.
  • A stock adjustment corrects the stock without a purchase. A positive quantity adds stock, a negative quantity removes it, and the reason explains why.
  • Both are stored in their own table. They never change the imported dataset rows or the manual sales records, and they are never counted twice.
  • Purchases and adjustments carry no opening stock or reorder level of their own; those come from the historical dataset, so a movement-only day reports no opening stock rather than zero.
