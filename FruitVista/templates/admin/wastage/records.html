What these records mean

  • A Historical row comes from the imported synthetic dataset. Its damaged quantity and wastage cost are the stored values, and the cost per kilogram is worked out as wastage cost ÷ damaged quantity.
  • A Manual row was recorded in this module. It can be edited here; a historical row is never changed.
  • Historical and manual wastage are added together once, so the same loss is never counted twice.
