Synthetic historical dataset. Every sales, stock, wastage and price figure in FruitVista comes from a generated synthetic dataset covering 2022–2026, used to demonstrate the analytics and prediction workflow. It is not verified real-world market data.

Experimental estimates. The machine-learning price, sales and demand forecasts are experimental estimates produced from that synthetic dataset. They are not verified market forecasts and must not be used for real trading decisions.

What the platform does

Four pillars of the FruitVista platform

Every pillar reads from the same SQLite database, so historical imports and manually entered records always produce consistent results.

Sales Analytics

Quantities sold, revenue, demand and availability percentages broken down by district, fruit, category and date range.

Inventory Management

Opening, purchased and closing stock with reorder levels and low-stock alerts, alongside damaged quantity and wastage cost.

Price Prediction

Machine-learning models trained on the stored history estimate future selling prices per kilogram, so purchases can be planned ahead.

Experimental estimates

Market Insights

Plain-language observations, seasonal patterns and five-district comparisons generated from the figures the other modules publish.
